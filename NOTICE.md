## Algorithm provenance

- `retro-core::rng::GlibcRand` is an independent clean-room reimplementation of the additive-feedback generator described by the reference implementation in glibc's `stdlib/random_r.c` (TYPE_3, degree 31, separation 3). glibc is distributed under the GNU Lesser General Public License v2.1 or later; no glibc source code is copied into this repository, only the documented algorithm and its observable output (verified against a locally compiled `srand(1); rand()` known-answer program).
- `retro-core::rng::RandSeeded` follows the seeded LCG and 3-round xor mix in `RSDKv5-Decompilation` (`RSDKv5/RSDK/Core/Math.hpp`).
- `retro-core::math` lookup tables follow the table construction in `RSDKv4-Decompilation` (`RSDKv4/Math.cpp`) and are regenerated by `tools/gen_math_tables.sh`.
- `retro-core::color::rgb888_to_rgb5551` follows `RGB888_TO_RGB5551` in `RSDKv4-Decompilation` (`RSDKv4/Palette.hpp`).
